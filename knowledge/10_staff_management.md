## Staff Management > Overview > Staff Roles

JAMM PX has three staff roles. Each role controls what a staff member can see and do inside the application.

Firm Owner has full access to everything in the firm. A firm owner can manage firm settings, billing, and add or remove staff members. Only firm owners can invite new staff.

Manager has access to all client and engagement data. A manager can view and edit all clients, engagements, tasks, invoices, and time entries across the firm. A manager cannot change firm settings or billing.

Staff has access to assigned engagements and tasks only. A staff member cannot see engagements they are not assigned to. Staff members see only their own time entries by default.

---

## Staff Management > Overview > What Each Role Can and Cannot Do

Firm Owner can do everything: invite staff, change roles, view all data, manage billing, configure settings, access IRS authorizations, and send magic links to clients.

Manager can view and edit all client and engagement data, send document requests, create invoices, manage tasks, approve time entries, and send magic links to clients. A manager cannot invite staff, remove staff, or change firm settings.

Staff can view and work on engagements and tasks assigned to them. Staff can log time entries against their assigned engagements. Staff cannot see engagements they are not assigned to, cannot view IRS authorizations, and cannot access billing or settings.

---

## Staff Management > Inviting Staff > How to Invite a Staff Member

Navigate to Settings in the left sidebar and select Team. Enter the new staff member's email address and select their role. Click Invite. JAMM PX sends the staff member an email with a magic link to set their password and activate their account.

Only firm owners can invite staff. Managers and staff cannot add new team members.

The staff member must click the link in the invitation email to activate their account. Until they accept, their invitation shows as pending in the team list.

---

## Staff Management > Inviting Staff > Pending Invitations

A pending invitation means the staff member has been invited but has not yet clicked the link and activated their account. Navigate to Settings and select Team to see pending invitations.

If a staff member has not received the invitation email, check their spam folder first. If the link has expired, remove the pending invitation and re-invite using the same email address.

---

## Staff Management > Managing Staff > Changing a Staff Member's Role

Navigate to Settings and select Team. Find the staff member and change their role using the role selector. The change takes effect immediately. Any existing sessions for that staff member end immediately when their role changes, requiring them to log in again.

Only firm owners can change staff roles.

---

## Staff Management > Managing Staff > Removing a Staff Member

Navigate to Settings and select Team. Find the staff member and select Remove. The staff member loses access to JAMM PX immediately. Their historical time entries, tasks, and activity remain in the system and are not deleted.

Only firm owners can remove staff. Removing a staff member does not delete any data they created or were assigned to.

---

## Staff Management > Managing Staff > Staff Capacity Overview

The Timesheets page provides visibility into staff workload. Navigate to Timesheets and use the staff filter to view time logged by a specific staff member. Managers and firm owners can filter by any staff member. Staff members see only their own entries.

Use the weekly or monthly timesheet views to compare hours across staff members and identify who has capacity and who is at or near their limit.

---

## Staff Management > Staff Integrations > Connecting Email

Each staff member can connect their Gmail or Outlook account individually. Navigate to Settings and select My Integrations. Connect Gmail or Outlook using the OAuth flow.

Connected email accounts are private to the individual staff member. Other staff members and the firm owner cannot see a staff member's connected email. The firm owner can enable or disable email sync at the firm level from firm settings.

Email connection is used for behavioral signal tracking only. JAMM PX reads thread metadata such as response lag and contact frequency. Email content is never stored or read.
