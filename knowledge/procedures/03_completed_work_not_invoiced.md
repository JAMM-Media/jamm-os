## Procedure: Completed Work Not Yet Invoiced

TRIGGER: Firm owner says they have finished work and have not invoiced for it, or the WIP report shows unbilled time against a completed engagement, or the firm owner asks how to bill for finished work.

STEP 1: Check the WIP report.
Navigate to Billing in the left sidebar and select WIP Report. Review the list of engagements with unbilled billable time. IF the engagement appears in the WIP report with unbilled hours, proceed to STEP 2.
IF the engagement does not appear in the WIP report, the time entries may not have been logged or may already be billed. Navigate to the engagement and check the time entries tab directly.

STEP 2: Determine the billing method.
Ask the firm owner how they want to bill for this work. IF they want to bill from time entries, proceed to STEP 3.
IF they want to create a flat fee invoice, proceed to STEP 4.

STEP 3: Create an invoice from time entries.
Navigate to the engagement. Select the option to create an invoice from time entries. Choose the unbilled time entries to include. JAMM PX creates a draft invoice with those entries as line items and marks the included time as billed. Review the draft invoice, set the due date, and proceed to STEP 5.

STEP 4: Create a manual invoice.
Navigate to Billing and click New Invoice. Select the client. Link the invoice to the engagement. Add line items with descriptions and amounts. Set the due date. The invoice is created in draft status. Proceed to STEP 5.

STEP 5: Review and send the invoice.
Open the draft invoice and review the line items, amounts, and due date. Set the delivery method to portal, email, or both. Click Send. The invoice status changes from draft to sent and the client can see it in their portal. IF the firm uses Stripe, the client can pay online immediately.
IF the firm does not yet have Stripe connected, navigate to Settings, open the Billing section, and connect Stripe before sending invoices for online payment.
