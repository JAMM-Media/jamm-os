## Procedure: Client Cannot Log Into the Portal

TRIGGER: Firm owner says a client cannot access their portal, a client says their link is not working, or a client says they are not receiving the login email.

STEP 1: Confirm portal access is enabled for this client.
Navigate to Clients and open the client record. Check the Portal Access section. IF portal access is disabled, enable it and proceed to STEP 2.
IF portal access is already enabled, proceed to STEP 2.

STEP 2: Send a fresh magic-link.
Navigate to the client record, open Portal Access, and click Send Magic-Link. A new link is generated and sent to the client's email address on file. Tell the firm owner to direct the client to check their inbox and spam folder for the most recent email from JAMM PX.
IF the client receives the new link and can log in, END.
IF the client does not receive the email, proceed to STEP 3.

STEP 3: Confirm the email address on file is correct.
Open the client record and check the email address. Ask the firm owner to confirm this is the email address the client expects to receive mail at. IF the email address is wrong, update it in the client record and send a new magic-link. END.
IF the email address is correct, proceed to STEP 4.

STEP 4: Check if the client has a password set.
Ask the firm owner whether this client has previously set a portal password. IF the client set a password, direct them to the portal login page and have them enter their email and password directly rather than using a magic-link. END.
IF the client has not set a password, proceed to STEP 5.

STEP 5: Check email deliverability.
The magic-link email may be blocked by the client's email provider or corporate spam filter. Ask the client to check all spam and junk folders. If using a corporate email, ask them to check with their IT department. IF the email is found in spam, ask the client to mark it as safe and use the link. END.
IF the email cannot be found at all, advise the firm owner to contact JAMM PX support as there may be a deliverability issue with that email domain.
