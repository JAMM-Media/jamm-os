## Procedure: Sending First Portal Magic-Links to Existing Client Base

TRIGGER: Firm has imported existing clients and is ready to activate the client portal for the first time across their client base.

STEP 1: Do not send to all clients at once.
Sending magic-links to all clients simultaneously creates a large volume of incoming questions and calls from clients unfamiliar with the portal. Send in batches of 10 to 15 clients per day for the first two weeks. Start with the most engaged or tech-comfortable clients.

STEP 2: Confirm portal branding is set before sending the first batch.
Navigate to Settings and select Portal. Confirm the firm name is set as the portal display name. Upload the firm logo if available. Set the color theme. Clients will see this branding when they open their portal for the first time. Make sure it reflects the firm before any client receives a link.

STEP 3: Confirm at least one document request or engagement is active for each client before sending.
A client who activates their portal and sees nothing actionable will not return. Before sending a magic-link to a client, confirm they have at least one active engagement visible in the portal or one pending document request on their To-do tab. IF no active content exists for a client, create a document request or engagement first, then send the magic-link.

STEP 4: Send the first batch.
Navigate to Clients and filter by clients without portal access. Select the first 10 to 15 clients from your priority list. For each, open the client record, enable portal access, and click Send Magic-Link. The client receives an email immediately.

STEP 5: Prepare for incoming questions.
Clients who receive their first magic-link commonly ask these questions: the link expired (send a new one), they cannot find the email (check spam, resend), they cannot upload a file (see procedure 01), and they do not know what to do in the portal (direct them to the To-do tab and the Upload button on their document request card).

STEP 6: Send the next batch after 3 to 5 days.
Wait until the first batch has had time to activate and any initial questions are resolved before sending the next group. Continue in batches of 10 to 15 until all clients have received portal access.

STEP 7: Enable the portal adoption sequence automation.
Navigate to Settings and select Automations. Confirm the Document Request Reminder preset is enabled. For clients who receive their portal access but do not upload within 3 days, this preset sends an automatic reminder without requiring manual follow-up from staff.
