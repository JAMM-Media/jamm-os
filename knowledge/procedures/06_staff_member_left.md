## Procedure: Staff Member Left the Firm

TRIGGER: A staff member has left the firm and their engagements, tasks, and time entries need to be transferred to another staff member.

STEP 1: Remove portal access immediately.
Navigate to Settings and select Team. Find the staff member and click Remove. Their access is revoked immediately and all active sessions end. Do this before any other step.

STEP 2: Identify all engagements assigned to this staff member.
Navigate to Engagements. Apply the assigned staff filter and select the departing staff member. Make a note of all active and in-progress engagements assigned to them. IF no engagements are assigned to this staff member, proceed to STEP 4.

STEP 3: Reassign engagements.
For each active engagement, open the engagement and click Edit. Change the assigned staff member to the appropriate replacement. Save each change. IF there are many engagements, use the bulk reassign option: check all engagements in the filtered list and use the bulk action toolbar to reassign to a new staff member at once.

STEP 4: Identify all open tasks assigned to this staff member.
Navigate to Tasks. Apply the assigned staff filter and select the departing staff member. Filter by status to show To Do and In Progress tasks only.

STEP 5: Reassign tasks.
Select all open tasks using the checkbox in the table header. Use the bulk action toolbar and click Reassign. Select the staff member who will take over these tasks. All selected tasks are reassigned immediately.

STEP 6: Review unbilled time entries.
Navigate to Timesheets and filter by the departing staff member. Review any unbilled time entries against active engagements. IF unbilled time exists, navigate to Billing and run the WIP report filtered by client to identify which engagements have outstanding unbilled time. Create invoices for any completed work before the staff member's records become inaccessible.

STEP 7: Document the transition.
For each reassigned engagement, add an internal note documenting the staff transition date and the name of the previous assignee. This preserves context for the new assignee and the firm's records.
