## Procedure: Document Request Has Been Pending Too Long

TRIGGER: A document request has been in pending or partial status for more than 7 days with no new uploads, or a firm owner asks why a client has not submitted their documents.

STEP 1: Confirm the client can access their portal.
Navigate to the client record and check that portal access is enabled. IF portal access is disabled, enable it and send a magic-link. The document request becomes visible to the client immediately after they log in. END.
IF portal access is enabled, proceed to STEP 2.

STEP 2: Send a manual reminder.
Navigate to the engagement and open the Document Requests tab. Open the stalled document request. Click Send Reminder. JAMM PX sends the client an email reminder that they have outstanding items to upload. Check the reminder count to confirm how many reminders have already been sent.
IF this is the first or second reminder, END and wait 3 days before the next step.
IF three or more reminders have been sent with no response, proceed to STEP 3.

STEP 3: Review which items are still pending.
Open the document request and review each checklist item. Identify which items are still in pending status. IF any items are optional and the engagement can proceed without them, waive those items. Open each optional item and change its status to waived.
IF all remaining items are required, proceed to STEP 4.

STEP 4: Contact the client directly.
The firm owner or assigned staff member should contact the client by phone or email outside the portal to discuss the outstanding items. Common reasons for stalled uploads include: the client does not have the documents yet, the client is confused about what is needed, or the client's portal access is not working.
IF the client needs more time, update the document request due date to reflect the new expected submission date. Add an internal note to the engagement documenting the conversation.
IF the client provides the documents directly, upload them manually from the staff application by navigating to the engagement, opening Document Requests, and uploading on behalf of the client. Mark each item as approved after uploading.

STEP 5: Escalate if the engagement is at risk.
IF the document request has been stalled for more than 14 days and the engagement has a deadline within 30 days, add an internal note to the engagement flagging the risk. Consider whether an extension is appropriate for this client. Navigate to the dashboard and review the engagement in the upcoming deadlines list to confirm the deadline visibility.
