## Procedure: Monthly Bookkeeping Close

TRIGGER: End of month and the firm needs to complete bookkeeping close for monthly bookkeeping clients.

STEP 1: Confirm recurring engagements spawned for the month.
Navigate to Engagements and filter by engagement type bookkeeping monthly. Confirm a new engagement has been created for each active bookkeeping client for the current month. IF any client is missing an engagement, the recurring template may not be configured. Navigate to Engagements, select Templates, find the monthly bookkeeping template, and confirm recurring is enabled with the correct cadence and advance days.

STEP 2: Move engagements to active status.
For each bookkeeping monthly engagement in draft status, open the engagement and change the status to active. This signals to staff that work has begun.

STEP 3: Check document requests for source files.
Open each active bookkeeping engagement and review the Document Requests tab. Confirm all required source files have been uploaded by the client and approved by staff. IF any required items are still pending, click Send Reminder on the document request to prompt the client.

STEP 4: Review and approve time entries.
Navigate to Timesheets and filter by the current month. Review submitted time entries for each bookkeeping engagement. Approve accurate entries. IF any entries need correction, edit them before approving.

STEP 5: Update engagement status to in review.
When bookkeeping work is complete and ready for partner or manager review, open the engagement and change the status to in review. Assign the review task to the appropriate reviewer.

STEP 6: Complete the engagement after review.
After review is complete, open the engagement and change the status to completed. JAMM PX records the completion date automatically.

STEP 7: Create invoices for the month.
Navigate to Billing and select WIP Report. Identify all completed bookkeeping engagements with unbilled time. For each, navigate to the engagement and create an invoice from time entries. Review the draft invoice and send it to the client. IF the Auto-Create Invoice on Engagement Completion preset is enabled, a draft invoice was already created when the engagement was marked completed. Review and send it.

STEP 8: Archive completed engagements.
After invoices are sent and the work is fully complete, open each completed bookkeeping engagement and change the status to archived. This removes it from the active working view and keeps the engagement list focused on current work.
