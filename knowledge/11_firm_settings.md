## Firm Settings > Overview > How to Access Settings

Navigate to Settings in the left sidebar to open the settings area. Settings are organized into sections: Account, Firm Settings, Portal, Email, and Data.

Account contains My Integrations, Profile, and Security. Firm Settings contains Firm, Team, Automations, and Fee Schedule. Portal contains Portal branding and Portal Domain. Email contains Email Domain and Email and Calendar. Data contains Migration.

Most firm-level settings are accessible to firm owners only. Staff and managers see a limited view of settings relevant to their account.

---

## Firm Settings > Firm Profile > Firm Name and Details

Navigate to Settings and select the Firm tab. Update the firm name, contact details, and other firm-level information here.

The firm name appears throughout the application and in client-facing communications including emails and the client portal top bar.

---

## Firm Settings > Fee Schedule > What the Fee Schedule Is

The fee schedule stores the firm's base rates for each engagement type. Setting up a fee schedule allows JAMM PX to calculate suggested fees for new engagements and to identify pricing gaps between what was charged and what the schedule says should have been charged.

Navigate to Settings and select Fee Schedule.

---

## Firm Settings > Fee Schedule > Setting Base Rates

The fee schedule lists every engagement type supported by JAMM PX. For each type, enter the base fee your firm charges. Types include all tax return forms (1040, 1120, 1120-S, 1065, 1041, 706, 1040-X), extensions (4868, 7004, 8868), bookkeeping monthly and quarterly, quarterly payroll 941, tax planning and advisory, audit representation, and custom.

Leave a field blank for engagement types your firm does not offer.

---

## Firm Settings > Fee Schedule > Complexity Adders

Complexity adders are additional fees on top of the base rate for engagements that involve extra work. The fee schedule supports two types of adders.

Fixed adders add a flat dollar amount to the base fee when a complexity flag is set on an engagement. Fixed adder flags include rental property, foreign accounts and FBAR, depreciation schedules, home office deduction, multiple states, trust or estate involvement, business sale or disposition, and equity compensation.

Tiered adders add a variable amount based on quantity or scope. Tiered adder flags are K-1 involvement and cryptocurrency transactions. For each tier, enter a label and a dollar amount. Example tiers for K-1: one to five K-1s at a set rate, six to ten K-1s at a higher rate.

Set complexity adder amounts in the Fee Schedule tab. The adders apply automatically when those flags are set on an engagement.

## Firm Settings > Security > Password Policy

Navigate to Settings and select Security. The Security tab lets the firm owner configure the password policy for all staff accounts.

Set the minimum password length and complexity requirements. Changes apply to all staff members at their next password reset.

---

## Firm Settings > Security > Session Timeout

Navigate to Settings and select Security. Set the session timeout duration. Staff members are automatically logged out after being inactive for the configured period.

---

## Firm Settings > Security > Failed Login Alert

Navigate to Settings and select Security. Enable the failed login alert to receive a notification when a staff account has multiple failed login attempts. This helps identify potential unauthorized access attempts.

---

## Firm Settings > Portal Branding > Customizing the Client Portal

Navigate to Settings and select Portal. Upload a firm logo, set the portal display name, and configure the color theme.

The portal display name appears in the top bar of the client portal. If no logo is uploaded, the display name appears as text. The color theme controls the top bar color, accent color, and other portal colors.

Changes apply to the portal for all clients immediately after saving.

---

## Firm Settings > Email Domain > Custom Sending Domain

Navigate to Settings and select Email Domain. Configure a custom sending domain so that automated emails sent by JAMM PX appear to come from your firm's own domain rather than a generic address.

Setting up a custom sending domain requires adding DNS records to your domain. JAMM PX provides the required DNS values. Add those records in your domain registrar and verify the domain in settings.

---

## Firm Settings > Portal Domain > Custom Portal Domain

Navigate to Settings and select Portal Domain. Configure a custom domain for the client portal so clients access it at a URL that uses your firm's domain rather than the default JAMM PX address.

Setting up a custom portal domain requires adding a DNS CNAME record. JAMM PX provides the required value. Add the record in your domain registrar and verify in settings.

---

## Firm Settings > Data Export > Exporting All Firm Data

Navigate to Settings. The data export option generates a ZIP file containing CSVs for all firm data including clients, engagements, invoices, tasks, IRS authorizations, notes, time entries, and documents.

Only firm owners can trigger a data export. The export is available from the firm settings page. Use the export for backup purposes, migration to another system, or regulatory requirements.

---

## Firm Settings > Migration > Importing from Another System

Navigate to Settings and select Migration. The Migration tab supports importing client and engagement data from TaxDome and from CSV files.

For TaxDome imports, export your data from TaxDome in their standard export format and upload the file in the Migration tab. JAMM PX maps the fields automatically and shows a preview before confirming the import.

For CSV imports, use the CSV import wizard which walks through field mapping, preview, and confirmation. The wizard supports client records and basic engagement data.

Imports do not overwrite existing records. JAMM PX checks for duplicates before importing and shows any conflicts for review.
